nmcli  -  NetworkManager 命令行
═══ 常用用法 ═══
常用指令集:
  nmcli device status            # 设备状态
  nmcli radio wifi on/off        # WiFi开关
  nmcli device wifi list         # 扫描WiFi
  nmcli device wifi connect "SSID" password "密码"
  nmcli connection show          # 连接列表
  nmcli connection up/down 连接名
  nmcli connection delete 连接名
  nmcli general status           # 总体状态
